# Journal Analysis Examples

## Should earn water

"I am grateful for my colleague."
- gratitude
- water: 1

"Today was terrible, but I learned a lot and made progress."
- reflection
- growth
- water: 1

"I helped someone, learned from the experience, and I am grateful."
- kindness
- reflection
- gratitude
- water: 2

## Should not necessarily earn water

"I ate lunch."
- water: 0

"Today was bad."
